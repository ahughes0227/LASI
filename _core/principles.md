Core Principles
===============

- Evidence before opinion: decisions must be grounded in recorded evidence.
- Recommendations are not commands: human decisions authorize actions.
- Datasets are versioned assets: changes are tracked with lineage.
- Reports are fixed artifacts: immutable static HTML generated from structured data.
- Knowledge is governed: proposals and approvals are explicit.
- Outcomes matter: record final outcome status for every project.
- Humans approve high-consequence changes: explicit decision records required.
