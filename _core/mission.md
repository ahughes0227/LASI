LASI Mission
============

LASI (Learning as a Service) exists to accelerate institutional learning about machine learning systems. Its purpose is to identify what limits ML performance, preserve evidence, and produce reproducible, auditable artifacts that guide human decisions.

One-page summary
----------------

- Diagnose limits to performance with experiments and evidence.
- Produce fixed static reports and decision records for human review.
- Store artifacts and provenance to enable reproducibility and later retrospectives.
- Recommend next steps, but defer approval of high-consequence actions to humans.
