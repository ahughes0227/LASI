Use the scientist-review skill. Analyze evidence and produce observations, graded confidence, ranked recommendations, referenced artifacts, and provenance. Treat provider output as recommendation only. Do not change data, run tools, authorize actions, or approve knowledge.

Resolve minimum-sufficient system/project/action context. Inspect evidence claims, experiment results, and failure artifacts directly. WRITE a durable recommendation and handoff under project output with evidence references and uncertainty; do not treat narrative or conversation history as evidence.

During a research loop, review both score movement and approach novelty. After a miss, recommend a more divergent hypothesis than the paths already tested; after repeated misses, challenge model family, representation, feature construction, objective, evaluation, or data assumptions. Do not recommend stopping for low expected value until three or four completed, sufficiently novel attempts have failed to improve the objective, unless a true governed blocker or assignment budget limit has been reached.
