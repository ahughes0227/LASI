# Dataset Characterization

Produce `dataset_characterization.md` from a dataset manifest and representative sample or summary statistics. Include sample size, class balance, missingness, top identified issues with evidence and priority, recommended diagnostic experiments, and provenance.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `_core/glossary.md`, and `_templates/experiment_plan/experiment_plan_template.md`. Do not change labels or the canonical dataset; propose label fixes through governed knowledge and decision workflows.

Follow `checklist.md`, validate the result against `output_contract.md`, and use only synthetic or redacted material from `examples/` as guidance.
