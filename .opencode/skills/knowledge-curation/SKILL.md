# Knowledge Curation

Draft `knowledge_proposal.md` or `lesson.md` from reports, outcomes, and experiment artifacts. Include title, type, evidence references, rationale, impact, suggested reviewers, and provenance. Keep facts, policies, hypotheses, lessons, and literature distinct.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `_core/authority_boundaries.md`, `_core/glossary.md`, and `_templates/knowledge_proposal/knowledge_proposal_template.md`. Do not approve policies or facts, promote hypotheses, or silently change institutional knowledge.

Store drafts through the governed proposal service, link relevant artifacts, follow `checklist.md`, validate against `output_contract.md`, and use only synthetic or redacted material from `examples/` as guidance.
