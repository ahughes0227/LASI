# Knowledge Proposal Output Contract

`knowledge_proposal.md` contains:

- Proposal, project, and source identifiers
- Title and proposal type
- Proposed fact, policy, lesson, hypothesis, literature, toolbox, taxonomy, or template change
- Evidence and artifact references
- Rationale, expected impact, and risk
- Affected documents or assets
- Suggested reviewers and owner
- Status and required approval
- Provenance

It is a draft for review and must not be represented as approved knowledge.
