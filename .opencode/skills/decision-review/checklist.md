Decision Review Checklist
=========================

1. Gather proposal and `scientist_review.md`.
2. Record one canonical decision: allow, allow_with_warning, block, escalate_for_approval, convert_to_proposal, request_clarification, stop_project, or defer.
3. Attach rationale, scope, and expiration (if any).
4. Save `decision_record.md` and notify stakeholders.
