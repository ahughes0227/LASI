# Decision Review

Record `decision_record.md` with decision, allowed state, risk, rationale, scope, approver, approval dates, blocked items, missing inputs, policy/privacy/budget/remote checks, conditions, next action, handoff target, and provenance.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `_architecture/08_DECISION_SYSTEM.md`, `_core/authority_boundaries.md`, and `_templates/decision_record/decision_record_template.md`. Decision agents do not unilaterally run high-consequence actions; they record authorization, blocking, escalation, proposal, or clarification.

Follow `checklist.md`, validate against `output_contract.md` and the canonical schema, and use `examples/` only as synthetic guidance.
