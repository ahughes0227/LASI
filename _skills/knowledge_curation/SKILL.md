Skill: Knowledge Curation
=========================

Purpose
-------

Draft lessons, proposals, and knowledge documents derived from completed projects and retrospectives.

When to use
-----------

At the end of projects or during sabbatical reviews to capture lessons and proposals.

Required inputs
---------------

- Reports, outcome records, experiment artifacts.

Required outputs
----------------

- Draft `knowledge_proposal.md` or `lesson.md` saved under `knowledge/` for review.

Files to read first
-------------------

- _core/authority_boundaries.md
- _core/glossary.md

Forbidden actions
-----------------

- Do not approve policies or facts—create proposals only.

Completion criteria
-------------------

- A draft knowledge document with clear provenance and suggested reviewers.
