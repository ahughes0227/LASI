Skill: Outcome Recording
=========================

Purpose
-------

Record the final outcome of a project with status, rationale, and links to artifacts.

When to use
-----------

When a project reaches an outcome state (validated, deployed, failed, archived, etc.).

Required inputs
---------------

- Final artifacts, decision records, and report.

Required outputs
----------------

- `outcome_record.md` with status, explanation, and artifact links.

Files to read first
-------------------

- _core/glossary.md

Forbidden actions
-----------------

- Do not retroactively alter provenance or source artifacts.

Completion criteria
-------------------

- Outcome recorded with clear rationale and links.
